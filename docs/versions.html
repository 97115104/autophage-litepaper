About this page

The evolution of an idea from concept to living implementation. This page documents the complete development history of the Autophage Protocol from its initial vision to the current mathematical framework.

v1.1 — Governance & Implementation Clarity

June 22, 2025

Major Corrections

Fixed Rhythm token decay from 2% to correct 5% daily throughout all documentation. Replaced fixed 1.78 energy gradient with dynamic 1.2-3.0 range based on system health. Removed fictional oracle network—apps ARE the verification layer. Apps stake USDC and reputation, not ephemeral tokens.

New Governance Model

Proof of Contribution replaces token-weighted voting (tokens decay!). Contribution Score from lifetime Reservoir contributions plus reputation. Marketplace proposals: stake tokens on improvements, get USDC rewards. Governance by results: 5%+ health improvement equals stake returned plus USDC bonus. Failed proposals lose stake to Reservoir. Natural selection: unused features sunset after 180 days with less than 1% usage.

Technical Architecture Clarified

Best mode implementation without chain-specific details. Core contracts: HealthToken, Reservoir, VerificationEngine, ContributionGovernance. Apps handle all verification—no separate oracle infrastructure. Privacy through ZK proofs remains client-side.

v1.0 — Dynamic Individual Metabolic Capacity

June 21, 2025

Revolutionary shift from shared supply to individual metabolic limits. Each person maintains 8,000 base capacity adapting to environment. Inverted Kleiber's Law: capacity scales as $W^{-1/4}$ with network size. Network scaling from 64× (pioneer) to 1.002× (global scale). Geographic equity through local density modifiers.

Genetic specialization system with meaningful strategic tradeoffs. Specialists gain 26-50% domain bonus for 17-20% capacity reduction. True scale invariance from 1 to 7 billion users. Local price discovery replaces global supply manipulation. Daily generation capped at 5% of personal capacity. Population diversity emerges through 75% group bonus.
