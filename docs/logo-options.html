Design Concepts

Entropy Farms (Happy Stack Calculus) logo concepts exploring digital decay:

• ASCII/Text-based: Degrading typography, bit rot, matrix rain effects
• Mathematical: Fractal patterns, quantum states, thermodynamic arrows
• Digital Decay: Pixel death, glitch effects, network collapse
• Abstract Forms: Erosion patterns, dissolving structures, particle dispersion
