About this page

A comprehensive bibliography of sources that informed the Autophage Protocol's design. These references span economics, biology, cryptography, and health sciences, reflecting the interdisciplinary nature of the protocol. Not all references from the litepaper are listed here for brevity, but as new research emerges and the protocol evolves, additional references will be added to this living document.
