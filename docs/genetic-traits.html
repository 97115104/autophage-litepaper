About this page

Just like people have different metabolisms, the Autophage Protocol lets you develop your own "economic metabolism" through genetic traits. This page explains how traits work, why they matter, and how to choose the right specialization for your lifestyle. For real examples, see the use cases page.

Your Economic DNA

In regular economics, everyone earns money the same way. Work an hour, get paid an hour's wage. The Autophage Protocol recognizes that people are different. Some folks are natural athletes. Others excel at meditation. Some thrive in group settings while others prefer solo activities.

Genetic traits let you specialize. Think of them like skill points in a video game, but for your economic metabolism. You start neutral and can evolve up to 10 traits that modify how efficiently you earn different token types.

How Traits Work

Each trait has three numbers representing your affinity for each token type. For example, "Early Bird" might be (8/2/1), meaning:

  • 8 for Rhythm: You earn Rhythm tokens super efficiently from morning workouts
  • 2 for Healing: You earn Healing tokens at slightly above normal rate
  • 1 for Foundation: You earn Foundation tokens at base rate

These are not just random bonuses. They represent genuine biological and behavioral patterns. Early birds really do have more energy for morning exercise. Night owls genuinely focus better during late hours. The protocol recognizes and rewards these natural differences.

Evolving Your Traits

You do not pick traits from a menu. You evolve them by burning Foundation tokens. This ensures traits represent real investment in your health journey, not just optimization gaming.

The cost structure:
- First trait: 1,000 Foundation tokens
- Second trait: 2,500 Foundation tokens
- Third trait: 5,000 Foundation tokens
- Fourth trait: 10,000 Foundation tokens
- And so on, doubling each time

Most people develop 3-5 traits over their first year. Power users might push to 7-8 traits. The exponential costs ensure nobody can buy their way to dominance. You have to earn it through consistent healthy behavior.

The Specialization Trade-off

Here is the clever part. Specializing in one area means accepting lower efficiency in others. If you max out Rhythm traits for exercise, your Healing and Foundation earnings suffer. This creates natural economic diversity.

The math works out so that specialists earn about 30% more in their chosen domain but 20% less in others. Overall, specialists and generalists earn similar total value, just distributed differently. This prevents any one strategy from dominating.

Temporary Trait Boosts

Life changes. Maybe you are training for a marathon and need extra Rhythm efficiency. Or you are going through a rough patch and need Healing bonuses. The protocol lets you temporarily boost traits using Catalyst tokens.

Boosts last 30 days and cost varies by intensity:
- 10% boost: 100 Catalyst tokens
- 25% boost: 500 Catalyst tokens
- 50% boost: 2,000 Catalyst tokens

You can also gift boosts to others. Imagine your friend starts therapy. You could gift them a Healing boost to help them through the first month. It is like bringing someone soup when they are sick, but for their economic metabolism.

Why Traits Matter

Without traits, everyone would optimize the same activities. With traits, the ecosystem develops natural diversity. Runners run. Yogis yoga. Therapists counsel. Everyone contributes their strengths.

This diversity creates resilience. If everyone just ran for tokens, the system would be fragile. But when thousands of specialists each excel in their niche, the ecosystem thrives through all conditions. Just like a rainforest needs both canopy trees and forest floor fungi.
