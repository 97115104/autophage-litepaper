About the Protocol

The Autophage Protocol is an economic system where tokens decay over time. Health activities generate new tokens. The decay forces circulation instead of accumulation.

Zero-knowledge proofs protect user privacy while verifying activities. A dual-chamber reservoir maintains price stability. The mathematics target a Gini coefficient between 0.32 and 0.38.
