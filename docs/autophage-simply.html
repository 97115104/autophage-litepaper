The Simplest Way to Understand Autophage

If the technical details seem overwhelming, this page explains the core idea in the simplest possible terms. No math, no jargon, just the basic concept that could change how money works forever.

Money That Dies Like Milk

Regular money lasts forever. A dollar from 1950 is still a dollar today. But you are not the same as you were in 1950. You are not even the same as last week. You need to eat every day. Exercise regularly. See doctors when sick. You are alive, which means you need constant upkeep.

So why does our money work like rocks instead of like us?

The Big Problem

When money lasts forever, people who already have it can just wait. They do not need to eat today because their money does that for them. Meanwhile, everyone else has to work constantly just to survive. The money piles up with people who do not need it, while people who desperately need it can never catch up.

Imagine if food lasted forever. The first farmers would still be hoarding grain from 5,000 years ago while everyone else starved. Sounds crazy, right? But that is exactly how money works.

Money That Expires

The Autophage Protocol creates money that expires, just like everything else in nature. Your workout creates tokens that fade in a few days, just like your fitness does if you stop exercising. Your therapy session creates tokens that last weeks, matching how long the mental health benefits persist. Your annual checkup creates tokens that last months, like the protection from preventive care.

Use it or lose it. Just like life.

Where Does Expired Money Go?

Here is the beautiful part. When your tokens expire, they do not disappear. They flow into a community pool that pays for healthcare. Your unused gym tokens literally become someone's insulin. Your expired therapy tokens help someone else afford counseling.

Think of it like composting, but for money. Nothing is wasted. Everything that dies feeds new life.

But Wait, I Want to Save Money

Good news! The protocol has something called Wellness Vaults. Think of them like a medical savings account that actually makes sense. You can lock up your tokens for 30 days, 90 days, or even a year. The longer you lock them, the slower they decay. Some even earn a tiny bit of interest.

Here is the catch. These vaulted tokens can only be used for health expenses. Doctor visits, therapy, gym memberships, healthy food, anything that keeps you alive and well. You cannot cash them out to buy a yacht. This is not another wealth accumulation scheme. It is specifically for protecting your health savings from decay while ensuring they actually get used for health.

So if you know you need surgery in six months, you can vault your tokens now and they will mostly still be there when you need them. It is like meal prepping but for healthcare expenses.
